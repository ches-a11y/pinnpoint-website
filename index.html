The A5 Print & Ship label

Reduce packing time by up to 85%

One duplex-printed A5 document carries the shipping label on the front and the packing slip on the back — turning the usual five-step labelling routine into two: print the one document, then peel and apply. Fewer steps, fewer mismatched shipments, and more boxes out the door.

  • Dual-sided in a single pass. Shipping label and packing slip printed together — no separate packing lists, no plastic pouch.
  • Fewer errors. Label and contents come from the same document, so shipments match.
  • Room for your brand. Use the packing-slip side for messages, promotions, and personalised offers.
