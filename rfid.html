RFID

Smart RFID solutions

RFID-enabled sheeted labels and documents, made in the USA. Each solution embeds a UHF inlay into the label you already print, so items are readable at scale without changing the way you pack and ship.

The range

RFID built into the labels you already use

Our RFID solutions embed a UHF inlay directly into sheeted labels and documents, printed on RFID-capable duplex laser printers. You gain item-level visibility without adding a separate tagging step.

Shipping label + packing list

The shipping label and packing list in a single sheeted document, with a UHF RFID inlay built in for read-on-arrival visibility.

Shipping & return document

An RFID document that carries both the outbound shipping label and the return label, so returns are tracked as cleanly as despatches.

Pallet label

An RFID pallet label for tracking full loads through the warehouse and out to the dock.

Powered by PIM

The Pinnpoint Integration Module drives the sheeted RFID range

PIM — the Pinnpoint Integration Module — is the software behind our RFID sheeted-label solutions. It merges your shipping and document data into a single duplex PDF, encodes the embedded UHF inlay, and connects to the ERP and WMS systems that hold your order data, so RFID labels are generated straight from the source.

Why sheeted RFID

Item-level visibility, without a separate tagging step

Because the inlay is embedded in the label you already print, RFID becomes part of your existing pack-and-ship flow rather than an extra process at the bench.

  • Made in the USA. Manufactured to a consistent standard for reliable encoding and read rates.
  • Printed on duplex laser printers. RFID-capable duplex laser printing keeps the process familiar.
  • Driven by PIM. Encoding and data are handled by the Pinnpoint Integration Module.
