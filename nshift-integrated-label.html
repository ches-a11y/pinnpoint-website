The category

What an integrated label is

An integrated label carries the shipping label and the packing slip on one sheet, so a single printed document does the work of two. It removes the plastic document pouch, and it removes the step where someone matches a label to the right paperwork by hand.

Sold as blank stock

The usual route. You buy sheets with a die-cut label area, then build a print template and keep it in step with every carrier layout change.

Generated by your software

The Pinnpoint route. nShift Transsmart produces the finished A5 document itself, so there is no template to build and nothing to maintain.

How it works

Three steps, inside software you already use

No middleware, no separate print application, and no proprietary hardware on the packing bench.

1

Transsmart builds the document

Carrier label data and packing-slip data are merged into a single A5 document at the point you create the shipment.

2

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a thermal printer?

No. It prints on any standard duplex laser printer, which is what removes thermal hardware and consumables from the bench entirely.

How is this different from blank integrated label stock?

Blank stock still needs a template built and maintained against carrier label changes. Because this label is native to nShift Transsmart, the layout is already correct and stays correct.

How much packing time does it save?

High-volume shippers report up to 85% less packing time. One duplex document replaces a five-step process. Basis: a five-step routine of about 45 seconds a parcel, reduced to about 10 seconds. Results vary with bench layout and volume. See the results from the packing bench. Or put your own volume into the calculator.

Is the material sustainable?

FSC-certified stock, and it removes the single-use plastic document pouch. Pinnpoint plants a tree for every box sold.
