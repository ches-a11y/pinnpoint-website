The range

RFID built into the labels you already use

Our RFID solutions embed a UHF inlay directly into sheeted labels and documents, printed on RFID-capable laser printers from our print technology partner Xerox-Lexmark. You gain item-level visibility without adding a separate tagging step.

Shipping label + packing list

The shipping label and packing list in a single sheeted document, with a UHF RFID inlay built in for read-on-arrival visibility.

Shipping & return document

An RFID document that carries both the outbound shipping label and the return label, so returns are tracked as cleanly as dispatches.

Pallet label

An RFID pallet label for tracking full loads through the warehouse and out to the dock.

Powered by PIM

The Pinnpoint Integration Module drives the sheeted RFID range

PIM — the Pinnpoint Integration Module — is the configurator behind our RFID sheeted-label solutions. It builds your RFID labels as a print-ready PDF, encodes the embedded UHF inlay, and connects to the ERP and WMS systems that hold your order data, so labels are generated straight from the source.

Why sheeted RFID

Item-level visibility, without a separate tagging step

Because the inlay is embedded in the label you already print, RFID becomes part of your existing pack-and-ship flow rather than an extra process at the bench.

  • Consistent encoding. Manufactured to a consistent standard for reliable encoding and read rates.
  • Printed on RFID-capable laser printers. Laser printing keeps the process familiar.
  • Driven by PIM. Encoding and data are handled by the Pinnpoint Integration Module.
