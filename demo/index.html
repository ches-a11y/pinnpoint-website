The A5 Print & Ship label

Reduce packing time by nearly 80%

Basis: a five-step routine of about 45 seconds a parcel, reduced to about 10 seconds. Results vary with bench layout and volume.

One duplex-printed A5 document carries the shipping label on the front and the packing list on the back — turning the usual five-step labelling routine into two: print the one document, then peel and apply. Fewer steps, fewer mismatched shipments, and more boxes out the door.

  • Dual-sided in a single pass. Shipping label and packing list printed together — no separate packing lists, no plastic pouch.
  • Fewer errors. Label and contents come from the same document, so shipments match.
  • Room for your brand. Use the packing-list side for messages, promotions, and personalised offers.

Print it at the pack bench — or pick with it

  • At the pack bench. Print as each order is packed: one sheet, then peel and apply.
  • Pick with it. Print when the order is released, pick the items from the packing list on the back, then carry the sheet to the pack bench and apply it. The sheet that guided the pick ships with the box.
