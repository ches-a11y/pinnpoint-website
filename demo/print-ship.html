The A5 Print & Ship label

One label. Shipping label and packing list, in a single pass.

The A5 Print & Ship label is an integrated label that arrives ready to use — no blank stock, no template to configure — printed on standard duplex laser printers. Deploy it today from nShift Transsmart, or from any system that saves label PDFs, with PIM. A sustainable way to cut packing time by nearly 80%.

Request a sample pack See how it works

How it works

One document, from your system to the box

The A5 Print & Ship label merges the shipping label and packing list into one duplex-printed document. Your system generates it; at the packing bench, five steps become two: print, then peel and apply.

1

Generate the document

nShift Transsmart — or PIM, from the label PDFs your system already makes — produces a single A5 document, ready to print. PIM is a free download and installs in minutes.

2

Print duplex

Print the document double-sided on any standard duplex laser printer. If you need one, contact your business equipment dealer.

3

Apply and ship

Shipping label on the front, packing list on the back. Apply to the box and it's ready to go.

Print it at the pack bench — or pick with it

  • At the pack bench. Print as each order is packed: one sheet, then peel and apply.
  • Pick with it. Print when the order is released, pick the items from the packing list on the back, then carry the sheet to the pack bench and apply it. The sheet that guided the pick ships with the box.

From five steps to two

Getting a parcel out the door the traditional way takes five separate steps. The A5 Print & Ship label collapses them into two.

One document, two sides

Front and back, printed together

The A5 Print & Ship label carries the shipping label on the front and the packing list on the back — imaged together in a single duplex pass.

Front

Shipping label

Carrier-ready and scannable, imaged in the same pass as the packing list.

Back

Packing list

Why choose Print & Ship

Faster, cleaner, and already integrated

Nearly 80% less packing time

Fewer steps at the packing bench means more boxes out the door per hour. Basis: a five-step routine of about 45 seconds a parcel, reduced to about 10 seconds. Results vary with bench layout and volume.

Built into nShift Transsmart

Sustainable

One-to-one marketing

The packing list is created for every order, so it can carry a personal message and QR codes: order it again, a matching product, or how to return it. No inserts, no flyers.
