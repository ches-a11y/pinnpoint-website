Pinnpoint featured at NRF 2019

January 2019

← Back to blog

Print & Ship on show at the retail industry's flagship event in New York.

Each January, the National Retail Federation's Big Show brings the retail industry together in New York to look at the technology shaping how goods are sold, moved and delivered. Pinnpoint used the event to put the A5 Print & Ship label in front of retailers wrestling with the same dispatch bottleneck.

One document instead of two

For retail fulfilment teams shipping at volume, the packing bench is where small inefficiencies multiply. Print & Ship merges the shipping label and packing list into a single duplex-printed document — turning a multi-step routine into two, and removing the single-use plastic pouch that usually carries the packing list.

  • Shipping label and packing list on one duplex document
  • Printed on standard duplex laser printers — no proprietary hardware
  • The single-use plastic document pouch removed entirely

The response from retailers echoed what Pinnpoint hears again and again: faster packing and less waste tend to come from the same decision, not from competing priorities.
