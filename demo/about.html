About Pinnpoint

Passionate about better logistics

Out of a passion for logistics and process improvement, we created Print & Ship — one duplex label that combines the shipping label and packing list, built into nShift Transsmart.

Our story

Built to take steps out of shipping

We started with a simple frustration: too many steps at the packing bench. So we created Print & Ship — a solution designed to save cost and time, and to scale from a small third-party logistics operation up to a global enterprise.

  • Duplex laser-imaged labels. Shipping label and packing list on one document, printed on standard duplex laser printers.
  • Built into nShift Transsmart. Generated straight from the delivery software our Dutch customers already run.
  • One-to-one messaging. The packing-list side leaves room for personalised messages and branding.

Sustainability

Kinder to the planet, by design

Doing shipping better should also mean wasting less. By printing the packing list into the label, Print & Ship removes the single-use plastic document wallet — and we build the labels with responsibly sourced materials.

  • Made with FSC-certified papers. Responsibly sourced stock for every label we produce.
  • A tree for every box. In partnership with One Tree Planted, a tree is planted for each box of labels.
  • No plastic pouch. The printed-in packing list removes the single-use document wallet entirely.
