The A5 Print & Ship label

One label. Shipping label and packing slip, in a single pass.

Built into nShift Transsmart and printed on standard duplex laser printers, the A5 Print & Ship label is an integrated label that arrives ready to use — no blank stock, no template to configure. A sustainable way to cut packing time by up to 85%.

How it works

Two steps instead of five

The A5 Print & Ship label merges the shipping label and packing slip into one duplex-printed document — turning a five-step process into two.

1

Generate in nShift Transsmart

nShift Transsmart combines your shipping label and packing-slip data into a single A5 document, ready to print.

2

Print duplex

Print the document double-sided on any standard duplex laser printer. No proprietary hardware needed.

3

Apply and ship

Shipping label on the front, packing slip on the back. Apply to the box and it's ready to go.

From five steps to two

Getting a parcel out the door the traditional way takes five separate steps. The A5 Print & Ship label collapses them into two.

One document, two sides

Front and back, printed together

The A5 Print & Ship label carries the shipping label on the front and the packing slip on the back — imaged together in a single duplex pass.

Front

Shipping label

Carrier-ready and scannable, imaged in the same pass as the slip.

Back

Packing slip

Replaces the separate packing list — with room for branding, messages, and offers.
