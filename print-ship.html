The A5 Print & Ship label

One label. Shipping label and packing slip, in a single pass.

Built into nShift Transsmart and printed on standard duplex laser printers, the A5 Print & Ship label is a sustainable way to cut packing time by up to 85%.

How it works

Two steps instead of five

The A5 Print & Ship label merges the shipping label and packing slip into one duplex-printed document — turning a five-step process into two.

1

Generate in nShift Transsmart

nShift Transsmart combines your shipping label and packing-slip data into a single A5 document, ready to print.

2

Print duplex

Print the document double-sided on any standard duplex laser printer. No proprietary hardware needed.

3

Apply and ship

Shipping label on the front, packing slip on the back. Apply to the box and it's ready to go.

From five steps to two

  • Fewer touches at the bench. No separate packing slip to print, match, and insert.
  • Fewer mis-ships. Label and contents come from one document, so they always match.
  • No plastic pouch. The packing slip is printed in, so the single-use document wallet disappears.
